Assembly scope

From the airframe to
the embedded system.

The service can cover a single integration, a platform under development or a complete assembly according to the agreed technical scope.

01 / AIRFRAME

Structure and mechanisms

Assembly of the airframe, control surfaces, mounts, compartments, landing gear, mechanisms and mechanical interfaces.

02 / PROPULSION

Propulsion and power

Matching of motors, propellers, ESCs, batteries, power distribution, connectors, protection and maintenance access.

03 / AVIONICS

Avionics and control

Installation of autopilot, inertial sensors, GNSS, telemetry, receivers, actuators and navigation peripherals.

04 / HARNESS

Harnesses and interfaces

Organization of power and signals, connection labeling and definition of interfaces between subsystems.

05 / PAYLOAD

Cameras and sensors

Mechanical, electrical and functional integration of RGB cameras, thermal systems, gimbals and other compatible payloads.

06 / SETUP

Configuration and checks

Initial parameter setup, calibrations and verification of commands, power, communication and functional behavior on the bench.

Organized assembly

Integration with access, balance and maintenance in mind.

The internal layout must account for center of gravity, cooling, electromagnetic interference, cable routing, mounting and access to components. Every assembly decision affects operation and maintenance.

  • Configuration survey and bill of materials
  • Positioning and mounting of subsystems
  • Electrical assembly and interface labeling
  • Record of the configuration and checks performed

Integration workflow

Assembly in stages.
Evidence at every step.

The process advances in a controlled way, with checks matched to the platform's maturity and the contracted scope.

  1. 01

    Mission and configuration

    Understanding of the use case, environment, platform, payload, intended endurance, interfaces and constraints.

  2. 02

    Architecture and components

    Definition of subsystems, compatibility, mass and power budget and integration points.

  3. 03

    Mechanical and electrical assembly

    Installation, mounting, harnesses, distribution, connectors, internal layout and accessibility.

  4. 04

    Configuration and bench

    Parameter setup, calibration and progressive checks of power, commands, sensors and communication.

  5. 05

    Planned validation

    Ground tests and other checks defined in the plan before any operational step forward.

  6. 06

    Documentation and support

    Record of the delivered configuration, agreed guidance and technical evolution as needed.
