Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Overview

Sirius Dynamics Technology respects the privacy of visitors, clients and partners. This policy explains how we handle personal information related to the use of this website and to contacts made with the company.

2. Data we may process

We may process data you voluntarily submit through contact forms, by email or via WhatsApp, such as name, company, email address, phone number, message and information needed to respond to a request. Forms are delivered by email through the FormSubmit service.

Hosting services and technical tools may also record basic browsing data, such as IP address, date and time of access, pages visited, browser type and device information, for security, operation and improvement of the website.

3. Purposes

  • Responding to contacts, proposals, questions and commercial requests.
  • Keeping administrative and professional relationship records.
  • Protecting the website, preventing abuse and preserving technical security.
  • Complying with legal, regulatory or contractual obligations where applicable.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information may be shared only when necessary with technical vendors, service providers, competent authorities or partners involved in a specific request, always in accordance with applicable law.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

The website uses essential technical resources and third-party files to load fonts, images, scripts and visual features, as well as a language preference cookie. We also use Google Analytics, Google Ads and Vercel Web Analytics to measure visits, understand page performance and evaluate campaign results, including recording form submissions and contact clicks. These tools may use cookies and identifiers. You can manage cookies in your browser settings and ad preferences at adssettings.google.com.

6. Security and retention

We adopt reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration or improper disclosure. Personal data is kept for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy or applicable legal obligations.

7. Your rights

Under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD) and other applicable laws, you may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, deletion, portability, information about sharing and withdrawal of consent where applicable.

8. Contact

To exercise your rights or discuss privacy matters, contact us by email at eric@siriusdynamics.com.br.
