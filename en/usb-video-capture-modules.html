Two modules.
Different functions.

One connects a compatible USB camera to the module's own Wi-Fi network. The other converts an analog CVBS video signal into a USB UVC source for the computer.

WFM01 · WI-FI MODULE FOR USB

Take USB camera video to a local wireless connection.

The WFM01 accepts UVC-compatible USB video devices and streams video over the module's own Wi-Fi network. According to the datasheet, no internet or local router is required.

  • InputCompatible USB 2.0 UVC devices
  • ViewingVideo stream over local Wi-Fi
  • Stated imageVGA at 30 fps, depending on the camera
  • Stated latencyLess than 0.5 second
  • Stated rangeOver 10 m in open space
  • ViewersMore than one user can watch the stream

Values according to the WFM01 datasheet; performance and compatibility depend on the camera and environment. To use it with Visus, confirm the stream URL and HTTP/MJPEG mode.

C8205 · CVBS TO USB UVC CONVERTER

Convert an analog signal into a USB input for the computer.

The C8205 accepts CVBS composite video and is recognized by the computer as a USB 2.0 UVC device. The video can then be selected as a camera source, subject to system and software compatibility.

  • Input signalCVBS composite analog video
  • Output formatUSB 2.0 UVC
  • Described connector3.5 mm jack on the C8205 datasheet
  • DetectionAutomatic PAL / NTSC
  • PAL resolution720 × 576
  • NTSC resolution720 × 480
  • PowerVia the computer's USB port

The C8205 datasheet describes CVBS input via a 3.5 mm jack and a USB connection to the computer. The image is a visual reference; confirm the housing and connectors of the unit offered. The datasheet states plug-and-play for Windows XP/7 and does not confirm current versions. Conversion does not increase the original resolution.

Does the WFM01 module need internet?

No, according to the datasheet. The WFM01 creates its own Wi-Fi network with an individual SSID. The computer or viewing device must connect to that network to access the video.

Does the WFM01 work with any USB camera?

The datasheet states support for most UVC-compatible USB 2.0 devices. “Most” does not mean all: confirm the camera model and stream format before purchasing.

Does the C8205 convert any analog camera?

The C8205 described in the datasheet accepts a CVBS signal via a 3.5 mm jack and detects PAL or NTSC. Confirm that your equipment's output and the unit's connectors match.

Are the modules compatible with Visus Pro 16.5?

Visus works with USB, HTTP Snapshot and MJPEG sources. Connecting the C8205 depends on the computer recognizing the UVC source; for the WFM01, the URL and HTTP/MJPEG stream must be validated. The exact configuration should be checked for the camera, Windows version and installation used.

Does the C8205 work on Windows 10 or 11?

The documentation received states plug-and-play for Windows XP and Windows 7, but does not confirm Windows 10 or 11. Please request validation of your current Windows version and equipment before purchasing.

Does conversion improve image resolution?

No. The converter presents the CVBS signal as a USB UVC output and states 720 × 576 for PAL or 720 × 480 for NTSC. It does not create detail beyond the original signal.
