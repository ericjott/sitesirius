of connected decisions.

The Sirius method

From the need
to a verified configuration.

Development advances in cycles of definition, integration and evidence. Activities and deliverables are proportional to the project's maturity and criticality.

  1. 01

    Mission and requirements

    Use scenario, environment, payload, constraints, interfaces, logistics and success criteria.

  2. 02

    System architecture

    Platform configuration, subsystems, mass and power budget and definition of the main interfaces.

  3. 03

    Engineering and prototyping

    Detailed design, component selection, manufacturing or adaptation and integration preparation.

  4. 04

    Integration and verification

    Functional checks, bench tests, interface analysis and test progression according to plan.

  5. 05

    Delivery and evolution

    Documentation, agreed training, technical support and an improvement plan for the next cycles.
