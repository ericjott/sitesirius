Engineering to reduce uncertainty

A prototype is not just a part.
It is a technical answer.

Before investing in product, tooling or scale, a prototype lets you test principles, interfaces, risks and architecture decisions. It turns a hypothesis into observable evidence.

That is why our work can start with research and feasibility, move through subsystems and reach a complete functional demonstrator — with the level of fidelity suited to the question the project needs to answer.

End-to-end integration

Value appears when everything works together.

An isolated board, an enclosure or an algorithm may work on their own and still fail as a product. An integrated prototype makes the interfaces between power, signals, data, software, assembly, heat dissipation, use and maintenance visible.

Research & development

Advance maturity with stages, evidence and decisions.

When relevant, the project can be organized using technology readiness references such as TRL, to make explicit what has already been demonstrated and what still needs validation.

01

Opportunity

Problem, application, hypothesis and expected value.

02

Proof of concept

Critical principle demonstrated in a controlled scenario.

03

Functional prototype

Priority functions combined for technical learning.

04

Engineering prototype

Integrated subsystems with higher fidelity of use.

05

Transfer

Technical package and preparation for the next phase.

The roadmap is sized case by case. Not every project needs to go through every stage, and the reference to TRL serves as a planning guide — not as automatic certification of maturity.

The right prototype for the right question

Fidelity with purpose.

A good prototype doesn't try to reproduce everything from the start. It focuses resources on the risks and hypotheses that need to be resolved at that stage.

PoC

Proof of concept

Validates a principle, technology or critical function with the smallest configuration able to produce evidence.

FUNC

Functional prototype

Combines priority functions to test performance, behavior, interaction and architecture decisions.

ENG

Engineering prototype

Integrates hardware, mechanics and software in a representative configuration for more rigorous verification.

DEM

Technology demonstrator

Communicates the system's capability to users, partners and decision-makers in a relevant scenario.

Build to learn

Form, assembly and use are requirements too.

Physical development considers interfaces, fasteners, access, volume, protection and the manufacturing process. CAD models and prototyped parts help verify assembly, technical ergonomics and compatibility before more expensive or hard-to-reverse choices.

  • Enclosures, structures, mechanisms and mounts
  • 3D printing, machining and processes selected according to purpose
  • Physical integration of boards, sensors, connectors and cabling
  • Design reviews focused on assembly and manufacturability
Development method

Integrate with method.

The process combines research and engineering. Each cycle produces deliverables and evidence that guide the next decision.

  1. 01

    Explore

    Context, research, available technologies, hypotheses, risks and success criteria.

  2. 02

    Architect

    Requirements, alternatives, subsystems, interfaces and development plan.

  3. 03

    Build

    Models, hardware, mechanics, firmware and software at the required fidelity.

  4. 04

    Integrate and verify

    Bring-up, functional tests, diagnostics, fixes and consolidation of evidence.

  5. 05

    Transfer and evolve

    Documentation, technical package, next risks and preparation for a pilot or new iteration.
