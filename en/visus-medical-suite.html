07 / ISR VISION

Rigorous observation.
Precise recording.

The ISR concept — Intelligence, Surveillance and Reconnaissance — is well known in military technology. In Visus, it inspires how the visual workflow is organized: follow the signal, record evidence and review images within the context of the exam.

It is a methodological reference applied to clinical documentation. The software is not military equipment and does not produce automatic diagnoses.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Medical reporting software:
what you need to know.

What is Visus Pro 16.5?

It is medical software for capturing exam images and videos, organizing patient records, visual analysis and creating digital PDF reports.

Does Visus help create medical reports?

Yes. The system provides structured templates for procedures across five specialties and lets you review the document and preview the PDF before issuing it.

Which exams and specialties are covered?

Visus covers Otorhinolaryngology, Urology, Gastroenterology, Bronchoscopy and Gynecology. Procedures include, for example, videolaryngoscopy, cystoscopy, upper GI endoscopy, colonoscopy, bronchoscopy, colposcopy and hysteroscopy.

Do Visus or its artificial intelligence make the diagnosis?

No. AI can assist with suggestions when configured and authorized, but it does not diagnose or sign reports. Clinical evaluation and decisions remain with the professional.

How can I check compatibility with my camera?

Visus supports USB, HTTP Snapshot and MJPEG cameras. Request a demo so we can evaluate the video source and workflow used in your clinic.
