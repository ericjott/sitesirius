The Sirius method

From problem to technical report.

A reliable study takes more than running a solver. Our workflow structures the question, makes assumptions explicit and includes checks proportional to the risk of the decision.

  1. 01

    Problem definition

    Objective, acceptance criteria, operating conditions, available data and the decision the study must support.

  2. 02

    Model preparation

    Review or simplification of geometry, materials, contacts, loads, boundary conditions and the analysis plan.

  3. 03

    Meshing and solution

    Discretization strategy, solver execution, convergence monitoring and handling of nonlinearities.

  4. 04

    Verification

    Balance checks, mesh and critical-parameter sensitivity, physical consistency and model limitations.

  5. 05

    Interpretation and delivery

    Relevant results, an answer to the original question, design recommendations and next validation steps.
